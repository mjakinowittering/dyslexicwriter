DyslexicWriter is a quiet place to write, made for people whose ideas arrive faster than their spelling. Write it, hear it read back to you, and keep it as ordinary files on your own computer.

## Why this exists

Hello, I'm Matthew. I'm dyslexic, and for years I caught my mistakes by reading my work out loud, slowly, and usually just after I had sent it. So I made the editor I wanted: one that stays out of the way, reads **every sentence** back to me, and never keeps my writing anywhere but my own folder.

> You don't have to spell it right the first time. You only have to get it down.

## Three things to try

1. Press **Read aloud** and listen to this page.
2. Open **Settings** and try the _OpenDyslexic_ font.
3. Type anything at all. It saves on its own.

## Where your writing lives

Every document is a plain markdown file in the folder you picked, so you can open it in anything, back it up however you like, and take it with you.

| When you          | DyslexicWriter                        |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Write             | Saves as you go                       |
| Press Read aloud  | Reads it back, one sentence at a time |
| Rename a document | Renames the file on your disk         |
