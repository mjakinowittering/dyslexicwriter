The Lantern Room

The stairs to the lantern room were never meant for a person carrying anything. Twenty-two of them, each one a hand's width narrower at the inside edge, spiralling up inside a tower built for the light and not for the keeper.

Ada went up sideways, the way her father had taught her, with the paraffin can held out over the drop and her free hand flat against the cold curve of the wall.
